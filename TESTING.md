# Orbit: first tester round

Invite link: https://tylerrockytheo.github.io/orbit-app/?test=1

Purpose: find out whether people voluntarily return to a feed of publisher stories and videos around their interests. This is a test of the product as it exists today. AI chat connections and generated posts are not live features.

## Send this invitation yourself

“Hey, I’m testing Orbit — a feed that puts stories and videos around your interests in one place. Could you give it a few minutes on your phone? No download or account needed.

https://tylerrockytheo.github.io/orbit-app/?test=1

Choose your own interests and use it however you normally would. If you feel like opening it again over the next three days, do. Afterwards, tap Feedback and share your answers back to me. If you wouldn’t use it, tell me — that’s useful too.”

Do not send this automatically, create accounts for testers, or ask them for API keys. Invite individually so they know whom to send feedback to.

## Keep this round small

- Start with 10 people who already read stories or watch online videos, with varied interests. Include some who are less likely to tell you what you want to hear.
- Invite in two groups of five. Fix a serious blocker from the first group before inviting the second; label results by version if it changes.
- Ask them to use their regular phone browser and keep using the same browser. In-app browsers can block provider playback or sharing; “open in browser” is a useful recovery step. Record a failed first visit too.
- For the first three people, ask to watch five minutes of their first visit (in person or a call). Have them talk through what they expect. Don't direct them to a favourite post or explain confusing controls before they try.
- Let the next three days happen without usage reminders. A prompted return is a different signal from choosing to come back.
- After three days, ask once for feedback. A non-response is not a positive answer.

## Ask about behaviour, then intent

The in-app form asks whether they would choose Orbit again, why, whether they returned, what they would use instead, and what got in the way. It offers a preview, Share and Copy. Nothing is sent automatically and there is no central dashboard. Testers must send their report to their inviter. No login is required for feedback.

Follow up on any return with: “What brought you back, and what did you find?” For a no: “What did you use instead?” Don't argue or pitch a future feature in response. A self-reported return is useful but is not independently measured retention. There are no passive test analytics or visit counters in this build.

## Decide before adding features

These are proposed decision rules for this small round, not industry benchmarks or proof of product-market fit:

- Continue with one focused improvement if at least 5 of 10 invitees report returning without prompting, and at least 3 can name a concrete reason they chose Orbit over their usual alternative. Seek examples, not just “looks good.”
- If they like the idea but don't return, investigate the missing reason to open it before broadening the feature set.
- If opening, onboarding, relevance or playback blocks people, fix that and repeat a small round before drawing a demand conclusion.
- If most would rather use their usual app and no distinct use emerges, pause development and revisit the proposition.

Report numbers against all 10 invitations and separately against confirmed users. Ten people are directional evidence, not a reliable market-size estimate. Do not describe this round as a retention percentage measured by analytics.

## Known limits to disclose

- This is a mobile web preview, with no app-store download or offline installation support.
- Account use is optional. Guest interests and saved posts stay in that browser; signing in can sync supported preferences. Setup's finer interests and format choices remain local.
- The current feed uses real publisher metadata and external players. It is not automatically connected to private AI conversations.
- YouTube and other providers control ads, buffering, availability and embed restrictions. Playback stalled in the development cloud browser; real-phone first-visit checks are still necessary.
- Shared post links can expire when a post leaves the current feed.
- Feedback drafts remain on the device where browser storage is available. Delete draft clears the current local draft, not messages already sent through another app.
- No new paid services, analytics collector or feedback backend was enabled.

Keep the core feature set steady during the round. Fix blockers, record them, and learn whether anyone misses Orbit when they stop using it.
